# Changes — 2026-09-24

Opening Study fixes, found by a user report ("Trompowsky shows the 3.dxc5 chapter but plays a 3.Nc3
line") and then an audit that replayed all 160 lines of the 9 live repertoires the way the drill
does.

## Wrong chapter label

- The move panel took the chapter name from the run's start position, and chose the first chapter
  that contains it. Early positions are shared by every chapter that passes through them (all four
  Trompowsky chapters start 1.d4 Nf6 2.Bg5 c5), so 26 of 160 lines showed the wrong chapter name.
- The `line_attempts` analytics row picked its chapter the same way and was misattributed too.
- The session now records the chapter each run was actually dealt from (`runChapterId`) and uses it
  for both.

## Runs drifting into another chapter

- A position can have several repertoire moves (e.g. Averbakh: Nbd7 / h6 / Na6 / c5, one chapter
  each). Playing the primary move when the dealt line wanted another was graded correct, but the
  opponent's replies stayed forced from the old line. The run then drifted into another chapter
  under the old label and ended partway through. The same could happen at 28 points across all
  lines.
- The run now follows the move actually played: it switches to a deck line that continues from the
  new position, the label updates, and the dealt line goes back into the deck. If no selected chapter
  continues from there, the run free-walks the repertoire instead.
- The hint and the "Correct" message now go by the dealt line's move, not the card's primary move.
  Any other recorded move shows "Also in your repertoire".

## Audit results

- Checked for every line, every move: chess.js and backend positions agree, including the en
  passant field. Every opponent move is present in the recorded replies, every line reaches its end,
  and no move comment comes from the wrong chapter.
- Replaying every line while always playing the primary move: all 160 runs end on a real line with
  the correct label (18 switch lines along the way).

## Book study

Audit of Study from Book: the live book (506 items, 24 chapters) plus the saved user data for it,
checked against the code.

- **Move numbering on Black-to-move starts.** Move-tree plies were counted from 0 at every root, and
  move lists pair moves by ply parity. On a position with Black to move (150 of the 506 book items,
  and "Analyze this line" from a Black repertoire on the Analysis Board), Black's first move landed
  in the White column. The backend now numbers plies from the root position's actual move number and
  side to move (`rootPly` in `internal/api/handlers.go`), so odd plies are always White's. The book
  page's "ply N" readout stays relative to the item's start.
- **Moves panel ignored sidelines.** The book Moves panel, "Save line", and the per-move evals all
  followed the tree's main line. If you stepped back and played a different move, the board showed
  the new move while the list didn't, and "Save line" saved the old main line. They now follow the
  line through the current move (`activeLine` in `lib/chess/moveTree.ts`).
- Chapter 1 lesson 1 had no source-page entry, so the PDF viewer didn't jump for it; added.
- Checked and fine: item and chapter IDs are unique; every item sits in its own chapter; FEN and
  side to move agree; every solution is legal, with canonical SAN and real mates. Every progress,
  activity, and saved-line row matches an existing item and its real chapter, and the saved line
  replays legally.
- `buyc1-ch2-puzzle-5`'s FEN claimed White queenside castling with no rook on a1. Corrected to `K`
  directly in the production `books` row.

## Castling rights for set-up positions

- New rule: in a set-up position (a book diagram, or a Lichess study chapter that starts from a
  custom FEN), a king and rook still on their home squares always keep that castling right. A right
  whose king or rook isn't home is dropped. This is `chess.NormalizeSetupCastling`, applied when
  books load (`book.ParseAndValidate`) and when study PGNs are parsed (`repertoire.ParsePGN`). It
  never applies to positions reached by playing moves, where a king or rook that moved and came back
  has really lost the right.
- Effect on live data: 52 of the 506 book positions change, most from `-` to a real right (e.g. the
  "Basic opening principles" and "Gambits" diagrams can now castle). No repertoire chapter changes,
  so every opening-trainer card ID, and the saved progress keyed by it, stays identical (checked
  against all 9 live repertoires). All 506 book solutions still validate.
